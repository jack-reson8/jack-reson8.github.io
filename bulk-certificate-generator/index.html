FAQ

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your spreadsheet and background are read and turned into PDFs inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server, which matters for student and employee lists.

Which spreadsheet formats work?

Excel (.xlsx, .xls), CSV and OpenDocument (.ods). The first row is treated as column names.

Can I use my own certificate design?

Yes. Export your design (from Canva, Word, Illustrator…) as a PNG or JPG, upload it as the background, then drag the name and other fields into place.

Do non-English names work?

Yes. Text is rendered with your system fonts, so Chinese, Japanese, accents and other scripts appear correctly.

How is this different from Canva Bulk Create?

No Pro subscription, no 300-row cap, and your data never leaves your device. You pay only for the batches you actually need.
