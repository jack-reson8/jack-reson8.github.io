FAQ

Do users complain more about CapCut or Canva?

Canva has fewer unhappy reviewers: 12% of its recent reviews are 1–2★, against 46% for CapCut. Both samples are the latest US App Store written reviews, which skew more negative than star-only ratings.

What is the main complaint about CapCut compared with Canva?

Users of both apps complain most about price & subscription. The biggest difference is price & subscription: 14% of CapCut reviews vs 3% of Canva reviews are negative and mention it.

How was this comparison made?

We read up to 500 of the newest US App Store reviews for each app (CapCut: 2026-10-02 to 2026-10-18; Canva: 2026-09-23 to 2026-10-18) and matched 1–2★ reviews against the same complaint patterns. Percentages are of all recent reviews, so the two apps are on the same scale.
