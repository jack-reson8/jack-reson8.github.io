FAQ

Do users complain more about Instagram or Snapchat?

Snapchat has fewer unhappy reviewers: 35% of its recent reviews are 1–2★, against 53% for Instagram. Both samples are the latest US App Store written reviews, which skew more negative than star-only ratings.

What is the main complaint about Instagram compared with Snapchat?

Instagram users complain most about login & account; Snapchat users about ads. The biggest difference is ads: 5% of Instagram reviews vs 20% of Snapchat reviews are negative and mention it.

How was this comparison made?

We read up to 500 of the newest US App Store reviews for each app (Instagram: 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-18; Snapchat: 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-24) and matched 1–2★ reviews against the same complaint patterns. Percentages are of all recent reviews, so the two apps are on the same scale.
