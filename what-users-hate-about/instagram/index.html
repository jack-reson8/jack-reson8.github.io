Its all-time rating is 4.69★ from 29,660,349 ratings, while the last 500 written reviews average 2.62★. Written reviews skew negative on most apps, so compare the trend between versions rather than the absolute number.

How was this analysed?

Reviews dated 2026-10-27 to 2026-10-31 were read from Apple's public review feed and matched against complaint patterns (crashes, login, sync, pricing, ads, UI, updates, support and more). One review can count toward several themes.
