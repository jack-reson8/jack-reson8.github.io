FAQ

What do users complain about most in Strava?

In 500 recent US App Store reviews, the biggest complaint themes among the 207 negative (1–2★) reviews are price & subscription (75, 36%), crashes & bugs (40, 19%), customer support (32, 15%).

Is Strava getting worse?

Its all-time rating is 4.81★ from 379,801 ratings, while the last 500 written reviews average 3.14★. Written reviews skew negative on most apps, so compare the trend between versions rather than the absolute number.

How was this analysed?

Reviews dated 2026-06-30 to 2026-10-31 were read from Apple's public review feed and matched against complaint patterns (crashes, login, sync, pricing, ads, UI, updates, support and more). One review can count toward several themes.
