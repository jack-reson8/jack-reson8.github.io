FAQ

What do users complain about most in Strava?

In 500 recent US App Store reviews, the biggest complaint themes among the 215 negative (1–2★) reviews are price & subscription (77, 36%), crashes & bugs (39, 18%), customer support (35, 16%).

Is Strava getting worse?

Its all-time rating is 4.81★ from 378,770 ratings, while the last 500 written reviews average 3.08★. Written reviews skew negative on most apps, so compare the trend between versions rather than the absolute number.

How was this analysed?

Reviews dated 2026-06-21 to 2026-10-24 were read from Apple's public review feed and matched against complaint patterns (crashes, login, sync, pricing, ads, UI, updates, support and more). One review can count toward several themes.
